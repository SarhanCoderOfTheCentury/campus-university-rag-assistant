# Flaticon navigation icons

Conversation and source navigation use the official Flaticon UIcons regular-rounded `comments` (U+F4BC) and `books` (U+F28D) glyphs from `@flaticon/flaticon-uicons` 3.3.1. The original glyph shapes are preserved in a two-glyph WOFF2 subset to avoid shipping the complete icon font. Reproduce with `scripts/subset-flaticon.py` and FontTools/Brotli; the generated subset is committed and no Python/font tool is required for the Vercel build.
