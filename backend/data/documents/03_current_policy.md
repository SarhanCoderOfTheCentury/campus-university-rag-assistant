### Admissions rules
The regulations say admissions occur twice a year; applicants must satisfy the program eligibility criteria; certain pending-result admissions are conditional; academic originals must be verified; selection depends on entry test and academic record and sometimes interviews. On conditional admission, a mark sheet must be submitted within a month of result declaration; failure to meet eligibility cancels admission.

**Source:** https://www.dsu.edu.pk/admission-regulations/
**Provenance:** `admission_regulations` (source PDF; capture date unknown).


### Migration and transfer of credits
Only transfers from HEC-recognized institutions are considered, with prior NOC. A maximum of 50% of total program credits can transfer. Minimum original grade is C for undergraduate and B for graduate courses; transferred courses are excluded from DSU CGPA. Conditions and approvals are detailed on the source page.

**Source:** https://www.dsu.edu.pk/admission-regulations/
**Provenance:** `admission_regulations` (source PDF; capture date unknown).
