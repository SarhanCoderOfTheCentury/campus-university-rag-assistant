### Admissions application: online route
The source outlines application submission online: choose program and criteria, use the ERP application page, upload scans, receive payment e-voucher and then e-admit card, appear for admission test/interview as applicable, receive offer and submit acceptance and fee. An offline route was labeled unavailable “due to current Covid-19 pandemic situation”; treat that dated wording as stale and do not say it remains disabled.

**Source:** https://www.dsu.edu.pk/admission-process/

### Admission acceptance and forged documents
The source says applicants are responsible for supplying documents and states the university can take disciplinary action, including cancelling admission, where documents are fraudulent.

### Required documents and common admissions questions
The FAQ lists past academic mark sheets/certificates, IBCC equivalence for O/A-level or technical qualifications where applicable, provisional certificate, CNIC/B-Form, photos, awaiting-result evidence and test scores where applicable. It says there is no uniform (but dress should meet societal professional and moral codes); no medical or diploma programs; admissions are merit based. Availability of transport is noted through privately run vans and buses.

**Source:** https://www.dsu.edu.pk/faqs/
**Provenance:** `faqs` (source PDF; capture date unknown).
