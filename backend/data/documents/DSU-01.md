# DHA Suffa University (DSU) - Company Overview

Note: This is a fictional classroom dataset created for a RAG assignment. It is not official DHA Suffa University information.

## About DSU
DHA Suffa University (DSU) is a private university in Karachi, Pakistan. DSU was established in 2012. DSU has three faculties: the Faculty of Engineering, the Faculty of Computing, and the Faculty of Management Sciences. DSU currently has about 2,400 enrolled students. DSU runs two regular semesters every year: Fall and Spring. Each semester has 16 teaching weeks followed by final examinations.

## Purpose of the DSU Knowledge Assistant

## Campus Location
DSU has one campus: the DSU Main Campus, located at Phase VII Extension, DHA, Karachi 75500, Pakistan. DSU has no branch campuses.

## Office Opening Hours
General rule: DSU administrative offices (the Registrar's Office, Finance Office, and Student Affairs) are open Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. They are closed on Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays.

Exception: The DSU Admissions Office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. During the admission season, 1 June to 31 July, the Admissions Office is also open on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

## Library Hours and Fines
The DSU Library is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and on Saturday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The library is closed on Sunday.

Exception: During final examination weeks, the DSU Library stays open until 12:00 midnight on weekdays.

The DSU Library fine for an overdue book is Rs. 20 per day per book.
