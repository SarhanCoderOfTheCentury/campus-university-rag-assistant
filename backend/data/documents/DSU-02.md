# DHA Suffa University (DSU) - Programs, Fees and Student Services

All tuition amounts below are per semester, in Pakistani Rupees (Rs.), and exclude one-time fees.

## Program: BS Computer Science (BSCS)
Faculty: Faculty of Computing. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 133. Tuition: Rs. 185,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate (Pre-Engineering, ICS, or equivalent) with Mathematics.

## Program: BS Software Engineering (BSSE)
Faculty: Faculty of Computing. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 132. Tuition: Rs. 185,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate (Pre-Engineering, ICS, or equivalent) with Mathematics.

## Program: BS Artificial Intelligence (BSAI)
Faculty: Faculty of Computing. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 133. Tuition: Rs. 190,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate (Pre-Engineering, ICS, or equivalent) with Mathematics.

## Program: BS Electrical Engineering (BSEE)
Faculty: Faculty of Engineering. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 136. Tuition: Rs. 210,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate Pre-Engineering or equivalent.

## Program: BS Civil Engineering (BSCE)
Faculty: Faculty of Engineering. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 138. Tuition: Rs. 205,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate Pre-Engineering or equivalent.

## Program: BS Computer Engineering (BSCpE)
Faculty: Faculty of Engineering. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 136. Tuition: Rs. 205,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate Pre-Engineering or equivalent.

## Program: Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
Faculty: Faculty of Management Sciences. Duration: 4 years (8 semesters). Credit hours: 130. Tuition: Rs. 160,000 per semester. Eligibility: Intermediate in any stream.

## Program: MS Computer Science (MSCS)
Faculty: Faculty of Computing. Duration: 2 years (4 semesters). Credit hours: 30. Tuition: Rs. 150,000 per semester. Eligibility: a 16-year bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field with a CGPA of at least 2.50.

## Program: Executive MBA (EMBA)
Faculty: Faculty of Management Sciences. Duration: 2 years (4 semesters). Credit hours: 54. Tuition: Rs. 140,000 per semester. Classes are held Monday to Thursday evenings, 6:00 PM to 9:30 PM. Eligibility: a bachelor's degree and at least 2 years of work experience.

## Common One-Time Fees
These fees apply to all DSU programs. The application fee is Rs. 3,000 and is non-refundable. The admission fee is Rs. 25,000, is paid once at admission, and is non-refundable. The security deposit is Rs. 15,000 and is refundable.

## Service: Merit Scholarship
DSU gives a Merit Scholarship to undergraduate applicants who score 85% or more in the DSU Admission Test. The scholarship waives 50% of tuition. A student keeps the scholarship in later semesters only if their semester GPA is at least 3.50.

## Service: Hardship Fund
The DSU Hardship Fund is need-based financial aid. After a committee review, it waives between 25% and 100% of tuition. The application deadline is 31 August for Fall semesters and 10 January for Spring semesters. Hardship Fund recipients receive special late-fee treatment, described in the current Student Academic and Fee Policy.

## Service: Career Services Office
The DSU Career Services Office supports internship placement and CV workshops. DSU holds an annual Career Fair every March.

## Service: Counselling Centre
The DSU Counselling Centre offers free, confidential counselling by appointment, Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
