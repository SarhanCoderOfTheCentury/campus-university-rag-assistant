# DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy - Version 3.0 (CURRENT)

Version 3.0 is the current policy. It applies to all DSU semesters starting on or after 1 August 2026, including Fall 2026. It replaces version 2.1, which is archived.

## 3.1 Attendance
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 3.0 | Status: CURRENT | Effective: 1 August 2026

General rule: A DSU student must attend at least 75% of classes in every course to be allowed to sit the final examination. A student below 75% is not allowed to sit the final examination and receives an F in that course.

Exception: For laboratory courses and laboratory components, the minimum attendance is 85%.

An absence supported by a medical certificate is excused and not counted as an absence, if the certificate is submitted to Student Affairs within 5 working days.

## 3.2 Add and Drop of Courses
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 3.0 | Status: CURRENT | Effective: 1 August 2026

A DSU student may add or drop courses without penalty during the first 10 calendar days of a semester. After day 10, courses cannot be added, and dropping a course requires written approval from the Head of Department.

## 3.3 Tuition Payment and Late Fee
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 3.0 | Status: CURRENT | Effective: 1 August 2026

Semester tuition must be paid by the due date announced by the Finance Office. The late fee is Rs. 3,000 for each week, or part of a week, of delay. The maximum late fee is Rs. 12,000. If tuition remains unpaid after 4 weeks, course registration is cancelled.

Exception: A student who receives the Hardship Fund pays no late fee if tuition is paid within 14 days of the due date.

## 3.4 Withdrawal and Refunds
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 3.0 | Status: CURRENT | Effective: 1 August 2026

When a student withdraws from DSU, the tuition refund depends on the timing:
- Before the first day of classes: 100% refund.
- During weeks 1 and 2: 75% refund.
- During weeks 3 and 4: 50% refund.
- After the end of week 4: no refund.

The application fee and admission fee are never refunded. The refundable security deposit is returned within 30 days after the student completes clearance.

## 3.5 Academic Probation
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 3.0 | Status: CURRENT | Effective: 1 August 2026

A DSU student whose CGPA falls below 2.00 at the end of a semester is placed on academic probation for the next semester. A student on academic probation may register for at most 12 credit hours.
