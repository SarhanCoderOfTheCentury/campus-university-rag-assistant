# DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy - Version 2.1 (ARCHIVED)

Version 2.1 is an old policy. It applied to DSU semesters that started between 1 January 2023 and 31 July 2026. It was replaced by version 3.0 on 1 August 2026 and is not valid for Fall 2026 or later. Use this document only for questions about old or historical rules.

## 2.1.1 Attendance
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 2.1 | Status: ARCHIVED | Effective: 1 January 2023 to 31 July 2026

Under version 2.1, a DSU student had to attend at least 80% of classes in every course to sit the final examination. A student below 80% received an F in that course. For laboratory courses and laboratory components, the minimum attendance was 85%. An absence supported by a medical certificate was excused if the certificate was submitted to Student Affairs within 3 working days.

## 2.1.2 Add and Drop of Courses
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 2.1 | Status: ARCHIVED | Effective: 1 January 2023 to 31 July 2026

Under version 2.1, a DSU student could add or drop courses without penalty during the first 5 calendar days of a semester.

## 2.1.3 Tuition Payment and Late Fee
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 2.1 | Status: ARCHIVED | Effective: 1 January 2023 to 31 July 2026

Under version 2.1, the late fee was Rs. 500 for each day of delay. The maximum late fee was Rs. 10,000. If tuition remained unpaid after 30 days, course registration was cancelled. Under version 2.1 there was no late-fee waiver for Hardship Fund recipients.

## 2.1.4 Withdrawal and Refunds
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 2.1 | Status: ARCHIVED | Effective: 1 January 2023 to 31 July 2026

Under version 2.1, the tuition refund on withdrawal was:
- Before the first day of classes: 100% refund.
- During week 1: 80% refund.
- During week 2: 50% refund.
- After the end of week 2: no refund.

The application fee and admission fee were never refunded. The security deposit was returned within 30 days after clearance.

## 2.1.5 Academic Probation
> Policy: DSU Student Academic and Fee Policy | Version 2.1 | Status: ARCHIVED | Effective: 1 January 2023 to 31 July 2026

Under version 2.1, a DSU student whose CGPA fell below 2.20 at the end of a semester was placed on academic probation. A student on academic probation could register for at most 12 credit hours.
