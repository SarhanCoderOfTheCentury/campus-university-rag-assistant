# DHA Suffa University (DSU) - Operations, Procedures and FAQs

## Admission Procedure
DSU admission has four steps. Step 1: submit the online application and pay the application fee. Step 2: sit the DSU Admission Test, which has 100 marks and lasts 90 minutes. The passing mark is 50. Step 3: DSU publishes the merit list within 7 working days of the test. Step 4: admitted candidates pay the admission fee and first-semester tuition within 10 calendar days of the offer letter.

Required documents: a copy of the CNIC or B-form, matric and intermediate transcripts, and 4 passport-size photographs.

## Transfer Students
DSU accepts transfer students from HEC-recognized universities. DSU transfers credit for up to 40% of the program's credit hours, and only for courses in which the student earned grade B or better.

## Fee Payment Procedure
A DSU student can pay fees through the DSU Student Portal by debit or credit card or by bank transfer, or with a challan at a partner bank branch. The Finance Office does not accept cash. The fee receipt appears in the Student Portal within 2 working days of payment.

## Course Registration
DSU students register for courses through the Student Portal during registration week. The maximum credit load is 18 credit hours per semester.

## Examinations and Results
DSU publishes final results on the Student Portal within 14 working days after the last examination. A student may apply for a recheck within 7 days of the results. The recheck fee is Rs. 1,500 per course. The fee is refunded if the marks change.

## Student ID Cards
A replacement DSU student ID card costs Rs. 1,000 and is ready in 3 working days. A student who loses an ID card must report the loss to Student Affairs.

## IT Helpdesk and Passwords
A DSU student can reset a Student Portal password using the Forgot Password link or by visiting the IT Helpdesk, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. DSU staff never ask for a password by email or chat.

## Library Borrowing
An undergraduate student can borrow 5 books for 14 days. A graduate student (MSCS or EMBA) can borrow 8 books for 21 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I pay my tuition in installments?**
A: No. DSU tuition is payable in full for each semester. Students who need financial help can apply to the Hardship Fund.

**Q: Does DSU hold classes on Saturdays?**
A: No. Undergraduate and MS classes run Monday to Friday. Executive MBA classes run Monday to Thursday evenings.

**Q: How do I get an official transcript?**
A: Apply at the Registrar's Office. An official transcript costs Rs. 800 per copy and is ready in 5 working days.

**Q: Whom do I contact about medical leave?**
A: Submit the medical certificate to Student Affairs. The deadline is stated in the current Student Academic and Fee Policy.

**Q: What is the library fine for an overdue book?**
A: The DSU Library fine is Rs. 25 per day per book.
