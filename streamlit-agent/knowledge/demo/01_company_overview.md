# Noor Skills Institute: company overview
Status: reference
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Fictional classroom business
Noor Skills Institute is a fictional training institute in Karachi, Pakistan. Every business fact in these five demonstration documents is invented for this classroom assignment. It is not DHA Suffa University and has no connection with a real institute. The assistant helps prospective learners and front-desk staff find course information, admissions rules, exception procedures, and policy changes. It cannot enroll learners or confirm available seats.

## Opening hours and location
The admissions office opens Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00 Pakistan time. The office is closed on Sundays. The fictional teaching centre is located at Learning Plaza, Block N, Karachi. Office opening hours describe front-desk availability; they do not imply that every class runs during those hours. Course schedules are described separately in the course catalogue.

## Contact and services
For admissions questions, the fictional contact address is admissions@noorskills.example. The academic support address is support@noorskills.example. These example-domain addresses are placeholders for a fictional business and cannot receive real enquiries. Services include short technical courses, supervised laboratory practice, portfolio reviews, admissions support, and certificate assessment. Learners should identify their course when asking a procedural question so the appropriate rule can be found.

## Scope and privacy
The institute teaches adults and does not offer university degrees, accommodation, or employment guarantees. Staff should explain available learning support without promising a job or a scholarship. Documents contain no student records or personal contact details. Learners should not submit passwords, identity scans, bank information, or medical records to the demonstration assistant. The assistant is a knowledge lookup prototype, not a student-record system.

## Source ownership
The fictional admissions coordinator maintains the current policy document. The catalogue describes teaching content and published course fees. The operations guide explains requests and exceptions. When a question requires both a course fact and a procedural rule, consult the catalogue and current policy together. The overview deliberately does not state the director's personal mobile number; that information is outside the public knowledge base.
