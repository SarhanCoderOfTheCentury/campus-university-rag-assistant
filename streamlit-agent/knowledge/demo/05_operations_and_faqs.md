# Noor Skills Institute: operations and frequently asked questions
Status: reference
Version: 2.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

## Applying for a course
Choose a course, check its prerequisites, complete the application form, and submit the processing fee. Admissions reviews the form and confirms whether a seat can be offered. The course catalogue supplies tuition fees and learning content. The current policy supplies the processing fee and eligibility rules. To estimate Web Foundations enrollment charges, consult both documents because tuition and application processing are separate charges.

## Requesting a refund or deferral
Send a written request to admissions@noorskills.example with the course name and enrollment reference. Admissions checks the standard refund window and any documented exception. The teaching team cannot promise payment dates or approve exceptions in chat. For a medical-emergency deferral, contact authorized admissions staff privately. The public assistant should explain the rule without collecting medical evidence.

## Correcting an attendance record
A learner reports the session date and course name to academic support within 3 working days of discovering an attendance error. The coordinator checks the instructor's record before making a correction. A request does not itself alter the record or waive the attendance threshold. The threshold is defined in the appropriate policy version, while this guide defines the correction procedure.

## Unresolved timetable conflict
Two internal notices disagree about the planned orientation time: notice A says 10:00 and notice B says 11:00. Neither notice supplies a version, effective date, or scope that establishes precedence. The orientation time is therefore unresolved in this knowledge base. Staff must explain both claims and ask admissions to confirm, rather than confidently choosing either time.

## Deliberately missing information
The documents do not provide hostel accommodation prices, the director's personal mobile number, or guaranteed job salaries. These questions should receive the standard insufficient-information response. Course fees should not be used to estimate hostel prices, and employment outcomes should not be invented from course descriptions.
